# A Reviewable Path from Sources to Video

## 0:00 - 0:20 — Begin with reviewed material

**[VISUAL]** Show a reviewed source pack becoming a structured episode plan with narration, citations, visuals, and accessibility notes.
**[CLAIM planning-source]** Authored and AI-assisted inputs become the same reviewable EpisodeSpec before production.

**[VOICEOVER]**

An educational video should begin with material a course owner trusts. One-Click AI Video Generator turns an approved topic, paper, source pack, document, or storyboard into the same structured episode plan. Narration, visual directions, citations, pronunciations, and accessibility notes become visible before production starts.

**Delivery:** Clear, welcoming, and measured.

## 0:20 - 0:40 — Choose providers explicitly

**[VISUAL]** Route separate text, image, video, speech, transcription, and embedding tasks to local or hosted provider cards.
**[CLAIM provider-source]** Providers advertise explicit capabilities and execution locations, and restricted data cannot use hosted routes.

**[VOICEOVER]**

Each task is routed by capability instead of being tied to one AI company. Planning can use Claude, OpenAI, Ollama, or another compatible bridge. Speech, transcription, images, and video can also use approved local or hosted tools. The project’s data policy blocks restricted information before it can reach a hosted provider.

**Delivery:** Emphasize explicit choice and the policy boundary.

## 0:40 - 1:00 — Freeze what was accepted

**[VISUAL]** Show accepted provider bytes entering a checksum-bound artifact store, followed by a network-free frozen replay.
**[CLAIM replay-source]** Exact reproducibility comes from replaying verified accepted bytes rather than promising identical fresh AI generations.

**[VOICEOVER]**

Fresh AI output may vary, even when a provider accepts a seed. The workflow therefore records every accepted result, its checksum, model revision, request, and provenance. During frozen replay, model and network calls are prohibited. The system verifies and reuses the exact bytes that reviewers already accepted.

**Delivery:** Direct and precise; pause slightly before “frozen replay.”

## 1:00 - 1:22 — Keep people responsible for release

**[VISUAL]** Present three approval gates leading to a video, captions, transcript, bibliography, disclosures, and QA report.
**[CLAIM release-source]** RIT course pilots require checksum-bound script, visual, and release approvals and export an accessible review package.

**[VOICEOVER]**

Automation never becomes the release authority. A course pilot requires separate approval of the script and evidence, the visual treatment, and the final master. The resulting package includes captions, transcripts, chapters, bibliography, source coverage, asset credits, AI disclosure, and quality reports—ready for deliberate human review and manual Panopto import.

**Delivery:** Confident, professional, and evidence preserving.
